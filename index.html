Wig care tips

Keep your style looking beautiful

  • Use care products appropriate for the wig's material.
  • Detangle gently, starting at the ends and working upward.
  • Store neatly on a stand or in a clean protective bag.
  • Follow supplier guidance before heat styling or washing.
